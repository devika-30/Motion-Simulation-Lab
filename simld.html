Understanding

Project Description

This simulation demonstrates multiple physics concepts including 1D motion, train motion, lift motion, ball dynamics, and pendulum behavior.

Users can adjust velocity, acceleration, gravity, and pendulum length; observe real-time motion; and analyze results through dynamic graphs. The page is arranged as a virtual lab with each concept in its own section, making it easier to explain during a presentation or internship review.
