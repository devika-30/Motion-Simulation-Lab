Project Description

This simulation demonstrates multiple physics concepts including 1D motion, train motion, lift motion, ball dynamics, and pendulum behavior. It allows users to adjust parameters like velocity and acceleration, observe real-time motion, and analyze results through dynamic graphs, improving understanding of motion and related principles.
